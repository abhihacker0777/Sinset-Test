About

SINSET 2027 is an international multidisciplinary conference bringing together researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, industry professionals, policymakers, and innovators to address emerging challenges and opportunities in sustainable science and technology.

The conference provides a platform for cutting-edge research and practical solutions across climate change, environmental sustainability, clean and renewable energy, resource management, advanced materials, smart technologies, resilient infrastructure, and responsible technological development — directly contributing to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

"Join us on 27–28 February 2027 at Poornima University, Jaipur, to share transformative ideas and contribute to intelligent, resilient, and sustainable solutions."

The Centre for the Development of Physics Education (CDPE) was established in May 1978 by the University Grants Commission. This was a sequel to excellent developmental work done by the Department of Physics during the University Leadership Programme (1971-78).

The activities of the Centre are (i) development of new Physics experiments and literature, (ii) assisting other institutions under contact programme, (iii) fabrication and supply of equipment developed by it to academic/research institutions on no-profit and no-loss basis, (iv) development of teaching aids in the form of Computer and Audio Visual Programmes. The Centre has developed over 100 experiments and supplied Physics equipments fabricated at the Centre to many Universities and Institutions in India and abroad. These equipment are also being used in training programmes sponsored by ICTP, Italy, at the University of Dar-es-salaam, Tanzania, University of Maiduguri, Nigeria, and the University of Zimbabwe, Harare. The Coupled Oscillator was awarded First Prize at an International Competition.

The Centre has been supporting research groups in the Physics Department for fabrication of equipment and extending IT expertise for examination reforms and academic development. The Centre conducts workshops, exhibitions and training programmes for college teachers and also school children through INSPIRE Programme of DST, Govt. of India.

The Centre for Research, Instrumentation & Development (CRID) at Poornima University, Jaipur, was established in 2019 as a centralized research facility bringing advanced analytical, characterization, synthesis, and fabrication capabilities under one roof. It supports interdisciplinary research in physical sciences, materials science, engineering, and allied areas through facilities such as Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy (AAS), UV–Visible Spectrophotometry, thin-film deposition, Kjeldahl analysis, and 3D printing, enabling both fundamental investigations and application-oriented development.

CRID also conducts training programmes, workshops, and skill-development activities to enhance the research capabilities of students, scholars, faculty members, and technical personnel. Its open-access model allows external researchers and industry professionals to access the facilities, fostering academia–industry collaboration and knowledge exchange. The centre has supported research in areas including photovoltaics, nanotechnology, materials science, and renewable energy, while providing research exposure to undergraduate students and contributing to doctoral research. Through its emphasis on advanced instrumentation, innovation, collaborative research, and technology development, CRID serves as a key research and innovation facility of Poornima University.

The Faculty of Science and Humanities (FSH), Poornima University, Jaipur, is committed to excellence in education, research, innovation, and holistic student development. It offers multidisciplinary programmes including B.Sc. PCM, B.Sc. ECC, B.Sc. Forensic Science, B.A., M.A. (Yoga), and Ph.D. programmes in relevant disciplines. FSH promotes conceptual understanding, interdisciplinary learning, critical thinking, and practical skills through outcome-based education, well-equipped laboratories, MOOCs, certification courses, summer training, and a structured mentor–mentee system.

The Faculty encourages research, innovation, and academic collaboration with universities, research organizations, government institutions, and industries. Access to resources such as DELNET, IEEE, J-Gate, and Scopus, along with the Centre for Research, Instrumentation & Development (CRID), strengthens its research ecosystem. Workshops, expert lectures, conferences, industry interactions, and international exchange programmes provide opportunities for academic and professional development. Through an interdisciplinary and research-oriented environment, FSH aims to nurture scientific curiosity, innovation, research aptitude, and professional competence, preparing students for higher education, careers, and meaningful contributions to society.

Poornima University (PU) is a state private University in Jaipur, India established by an act of the Rajasthan legislature. PU was established in 2012 with a vision to create a knowledge-based society with scientific temper, team spirit and dignity of labour to face global competition and challenges.

PU is recognized by University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) and 12(B) with the right to confer degrees as per Section 22 (1) of the UGC Act, 1956. Its architecture program is approved by the Council of Architecture (CoA) and the hotel management program is approved by NCHMCT. Currently, PU has more than 8000+ students from all over the country enrolled in nine faculties and fourteen departments of UG/PG and PhD Programs in various areas such as Management, Engineering, Computer Application Sciences, Architecture, Design, Public Health and Hotel Management etc.

Jaipur — the Pink City of India — is among the fifty global outsourcing cities and the 10th largest city of India. Founded on 18th November 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, ruler of Amber.

The city is colourful with organized architecture unveiling royal culture, arts, and traditions as the heritage of Rajasthan — a destination admired by tourists from across the globe.

Jaipur is home to magnificent forts and palaces — the Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar — each standing as a timeless testament to the grandeur of Rajput architecture and the vision of its royal founders.

The city's iconic pink-painted walls, ordered by Maharaja Ram Singh II in 1876 to welcome the Prince of Wales, have since become a symbol of Jaipur's warmth and hospitality — a colour that breathes life into every street and bazaar.

From the bustling lanes of Johari Bazaar adorned with precious gemstones to the vibrant handcrafted textiles of Bapu Bazaar, Jaipur's markets are a celebration of Rajasthan's rich artisanal heritage — block printing, blue pottery, and lac jewellery passed down through generations.

A city of festivals, flavours, and folklore — Jaipur stands today as a proud member of UNESCO's Creative Cities Network, where ancient traditions and modern ambitions walk hand in hand beneath the golden Rajasthani sun.

Rajasthan — the Land of Kings — is the largest state of India by area, spanning a magnificent 3,42,239 sq km of diverse and dramatic landscape. It encompasses most of the vast, inhospitable Great Indian Desert — the Thar — whose golden sands stretch endlessly along an edge paralleling the Sutlej-Indus River valley, bordering the nation of Pakistan to the west.

The state shares its borders with Pakistan to the west, Gujarat to the southwest, Madhya Pradesh to the southeast, Uttar Pradesh and Haryana to the northeast, and Punjab to the north — a land standing at the crossroads of history, culture, and geography.

Beyond its formidable deserts lie rolling Aravalli ranges — among the oldest mountain ranges on earth — lush wildlife sanctuaries, shimmering salt lakes, and ancient trade routes that once carried caravans of merchants, poets, and warriors across the subcontinent.

The glory of Rajasthan is alive in its majestic palaces and towering forts — Mehrangarh, Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore — each a silent narrator of centuries of valour, sacrifice, and royal splendour. Its monuments stand not merely as stone and mortar but as enduring symbols of a civilization that refused to fade.

The vibrant culture of Rajasthan — its folk music echoing across the dunes, its vivid festivals painting the sky with colour, its artisans crafting beauty from every thread and chisel — draws innumerable travellers from across the globe, each leaving with a piece of Rajasthan forever etched in their heart.

Submission Guidelines

Full-length papers only via Microsoft CMT.

01

Originality

Submissions must present original, unpublished work. Double-blind peer review process will be followed.

02

Format & Length

Papers must be written in clear English and be 6 to 10 pages in length. Prepare manuscripts in MS Word format.

03

Structure

Title, Abstract (150-200 words), Keywords (5-6), Intro, Methodology, Results, Conclusion, References (APA/IEEE style).

04

Plagiarism

Submissions will be screened using Turnitin. Similarity score must be less than 10% and AI score must be 0.

05

Author Expectations & Attendance

At least one author must register and present the paper in person or virtually. Each presentation should be 10 minutes. No-show papers will not be considered for publication.

06

Full-Length Papers Only

Authors will receive notification from the conference once the technical programme committee has reviewed the manuscript. The committee will accept only full-length papers for review. Please note that only abstract will not be considered.
